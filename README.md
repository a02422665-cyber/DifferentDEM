# <ins> DEM Differences Lab, WILD 4930 </ins>
## Here is the link to my project: https://usu.box.com/s/4tjwink6qvtlxbh5ik872o3s7ge5llqw
#### This project had us visualize the difference of different sizes of Digital Elevation Models (DEMs).
##### There are many layers with different resolutions, layered on top of each other in the Fishlake National Forest in Utah.
##### The purpose of this lab was not only to visualize the differences, but to understand why we would use different resolutions to accomplish different goals.
##### The final result is that we get to see the area as a 10m DEM, a 5m DEM, and through Lidar.
